About this piece

Jorge Spiropulo’s “Tree 22” belongs to the Trees collection: a tree landscape painting for collectors of contemporary California art. Available for purchase at $3000.00 (shipping quoted separately).

Buyers searching tree oil painting California or Jorge Spiropulo trees may land on Tree 22. Size: 5 feet 3 inches by 4 feet. Brushwork is direct and layered, with color used structurally rather than decoratively. Themes include family, street life, music, and landscape—subjects that recur across Spiropulo’s career.

Moraga and Lamorinda-area studio practice, within easy reach of Oakland and San Francisco collectors. Paintings emphasize impasto, strong color, and figure/landscape subjects painted in oil. Email the artist through jorgespiropulo.com for purchase inquiries, framing, or studio visits.

— Jorge Spiropulo
