About this piece

Jorge Spiropulo’s “Tree 40” belongs to the Trees collection: a tree landscape painting for collectors of contemporary California art. This piece has been sold; similar works may be available—contact the artist for current inventory.

Landscape Tree 40 targets searches for tree painting, oil landscape, Moraga artist, and Bay Area nature art. Size: 24 inches by 18 inches. Composition balances drawing and painterly mass—readable at a distance, detailed up close. Keywords: tree art on canvas, tree art on canvas, Jorge Spiropulo trees.

Original art from Jorge Spiropulo, an Argentine-born painter working in the San Francisco Bay Area. Earlier exhibitions included Los Angeles galleries; current work focuses on private and regional sales. Inquiries about shipping, framing, and studio pickup in Moraga are welcome via the website contact page.
